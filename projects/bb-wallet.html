The system

Process

Drawing a tutorial
that has to explain itself.

01

Illustrating the cast

The script came ready, but the world around it didn't exist. I created the character and her universe from scratch: her afro, hoop earrings, rooms and phones. The viewer sees a real-looking person using the wallet.

02

Adding texture

Flat vectors get monotonous over three minutes. Grain and brush texture on skin, hair, fabric and backgrounds add warmth and give the eye details to find while the information sinks in.

03

Animating for clarity

Each movement has a job: a hand tilts the phone, a number snaps into place, a color sweeps across to close one tip and open the next. Motion works as punctuation.

04

No voice-over

Without narration, the picture has to hold attention alone for three minutes. I relied on pacing: short beats, big numbers and palette changes marking each chapter.

Outcome

Published
and kept in use.

The tutorial was published on Banco do Brasil's YouTube channel and website, and stayed there for as long as the product existed, as the explainer for anyone opening a bB Wallet account.
