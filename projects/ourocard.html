Ourocard Stories

A series of hand-drawn animated stories for Ourocard, Banco do Brasil's card brand: small everyday situations drawn in ink, yellow and blue, like notebook doodles. I was the 2D animator of the series.

process

Animating a doodle and keeping its charm.

01 —

A story in ink

Each episode is drawn in loose ink lines on notebook paper, using only Banco do Brasil's yellow and blue. The style looks simple, which makes it harder to animate.

02 —

Keeping the wobble alive

Hand-drawn characters can't move like rigged puppets, or the effect breaks. I animated gestures, reactions and lip-sync keeping the sketchy, imperfect line.

03 —

Made for the feed

Vertical 9:16, captions always on and pacing for social: a hook in the first seconds, the joke and the Ourocard solution.

what happened next

A team idea the client adopted.

It started as a project from our team , inspired by narrated cartoon stories, and was never published as a series.

The client liked the style and used it in a nationwide internal campaign. The winning employee's prize included her own story, illustrated and animated in the same style, published on the bank's internal channel.
