Frames

The transitions are the piece.

Classical statues, cut-out mountains, traffic lights, transparency checkerboards and neon overpasses. Each sequence has its own world, and the challenge is moving smoothly from one to the next.

Process

Simple moves,
hard transitions.

01

Reading the artwork

The visual world came ready: a vaporwave collage of statues, rocks, grids and neon. Before animating, I studied how the layers were built and which ones could move without breaking the illusion.

02

Subtle movement

The animations are simple: drifts, scales and light pulses. They stay subtle so the composition leads, and precise so nothing looks accidental.

03

The transitions

Each transition takes the viewer to a completely different world, so the motion, the timing of the reveal and the hidden seam all had to match. That's where most of the hours went.

04

Effects

Glows, grain, masks and checkerboard reveals look easy, but keeping these layered effects consistent in every frame was the technical test of this exercise.

Note to self

I only animated here, and it was still one of the hardest exercises I've finished.

It isn't perfect and there's a lot I'd change, but finishing it was very satisfying .
