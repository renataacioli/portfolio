Process

My part in it.

✦ 01

Joining the crew

Being part of the team of animators on this film was a gift. Working under a director, alongside colleagues I could learn from every day, changed how I think about animation as a collective craft rather than a solo effort.

✦ 02

Cut-out, piece by piece

The characters were built as cut-out puppets and animated in After Effects — rigging the parts, parenting the joints and shaping every move in the graph editor, so the handmade feel of the artwork survived the rig.

✦ 03

Animating with care

My job was to help carry a delicate story: grief seen through the eyes of a creative, stubborn, bright little girl. That meant animating with restraint, letting pauses and small gestures do the emotional work.

✦ 04

Where it went

The film traveled to 30+ festivals in Brazil and abroad — Niigata (Japan), Tokyo Anime Award Festival, Havana and the International Film Festival of India among them — and is now streaming on YouTube, Apple TV, Google Play and Prime Video.
