Case study · Banco do Brasil at Na Praia Festival 2025

A whole stage
in sync.

During the 2025 Italy season of Na Praia, Banco do Brasil invited the crowd to its space at the festival. I planned and animated the 18 videos that carried the invitation across the stage between shows.

ClientBanco do Brasil
AgencyDream Factory
My roleConcept and motion system
Year2025
Output18 videos in 10 formats

Stage mockup built over the technical drawing, 30 seconds.

Challenge

The stage had 18 LED surfaces in 10 formats, spread over more than 300 m². The piece would play in the breaks between shows, alongside other brands' activations.

Decision

I designed the whole stage as a single picture and sliced the files from it. A shape that leaves the arch shows up on the tower beside it.

Result

The videos ran between shows from 22 August to 7 September, on a stage that hosted more than 100 acts over the season.

From the festival guide to the render

01

Read the festival first

Na Praia 2025 was the Italy edition: arches on the stage, fans of colour in the identity. The sunbursts in the Banco do Brasil videos borrow that shape, so the brand looks like part of the party.

02

Write for the dance floor

The promotion was called Colecione momentos. Each video carries a few large words that someone dancing far from the screen can still read.

03

Animate once, export 18 files

A single timeline drives all the files, which keeps the panels in time with each other.
