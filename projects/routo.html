Case study · Routo.IO launch film, 2026

A launch film
investors watched.

Routo.IO sends every user to the right link. For the launch I wrote, designed and animated a 34-second film, and the company later took it into its investor presentation.

ClientRouto.IO
My roleConcept, script, design and animation
Year2026
Format16:9, 34 seconds
ToolsAfter Effects, Illustrator

The film opens on the problem.

Challenge

A new SaaS product had about half a minute to explain what it does and why anyone should care.

Decision

The film opens on a feeling people already know, the dead end of a 404 page. The product arrives afterwards, as the relief.

Result

It was delivered on schedule, ran on Routo.IO's social channels at launch and was reused in the pitch to investors.

Selected frames

Four scenes from the film.

From script to render

01

Start with the frustration

Broken redirects and wrong screens fill the first seconds. The viewer recognises the problem before hearing about any feature.

02

Borrow the brand's world

Dark interface surfaces, a faint grid, bold type and electric blue all come from Routo's own product. The picture goes from light to dark as the link gets fixed.

03

Cut for clarity

Every transition and type cue serves one sentence: a single link that takes each user to the right place.
